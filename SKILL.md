# Editorial Portfolio Design

Use this skill as a portable design direction for personal frontend portfolios and small creative developer sites. It is a pattern library, not a fixed visual template: preserve the new project's content, brand, and audience while applying the same editorial clarity and interaction quality.

## Required references

- Read [design.md](design.md) when making visual, layout, typography, responsive, or motion decisions.
- Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) when implementing the design in a codebase.

## Core outcome

The result should communicate the person's role, strengths, and work quickly, then reward deeper exploration with restrained interaction. Keep proof and readable content ahead of decoration.

## Adaptation rules

- Use the tokens and compositions in `design.md` as starting points; adapt color, type, imagery, and copy to the brief.
- Do not copy another site's code, text, branding, imagery, or distinctive composition one-to-one.
- Treat hover, autoplay, and scroll effects as enhancements. Every important action needs a keyboard, touch, or static fallback.
- Keep project and experience content data-driven so the presentation can change without rewriting content.
